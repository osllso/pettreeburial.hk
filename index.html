Context
背景

A city that loves its pets has no dignified way to say goodbye.

一個深愛寵物的城市，卻沒有有尊嚴的告別方式。

Two options exist — landfill and unregulated cremation — both at odds with HK's climate commitments
堆填與無監管火化，兩者皆與香港的氣候承諾背道而馳

Hong Kong's pet economy is forecast to grow from HK$1.44 billion in 2024 to HK$2.05 billion by 2030. Nearly 38% of households now care for at least one animal, with owners spending up to HK$490,000 across a pet's lifetime on premium food, advanced veterinary care, and specialist services.

香港寵物市場預計從2024年的14.4億港元增長至2030年的20.5億港元。近38%的家庭飼養至少一隻動物，飼主一生中在寵物身上花費高達49萬港元。寵物，早已是家人。

Yet when a pet dies, the choice is stark. Owners can surrender the body to a landfill system that classifies remains as special waste under Cap. 132, or use a cremation market where most operators fall below the 0.5 tonne/hour licensing threshold of the Air Pollution Control Ordinance — meaning zero mandatory monitoring of dioxins, particulates, or CO₂.

然而當寵物離世，選擇殘酷：以「特殊廢物」送往堆填區，或選擇監管鬆散的火化市場——大多數設施低於《空氣污染管制條例》的發牌門檻，對二噁英、微粒及二氧化碳的排放毫無強制監測。

An animal may receive premium care for over a decade. When it dies, the only options irreconcilably contradict the value placed on that life.
一隻動物可以獲得十多年的頂級護理。牠離世後，能夠選擇的，卻與這份珍視形成無法調和的矛盾。

This structural contradiction — between the premium value of a pet in life and its legal devaluation in death — is a primary driver of demand for nature-based aftercare. Natural Organic Reduction (NOR) addresses both the environmental and human dimensions of this gap.

生命中的珍視與死後的棄置之間的制度性矛盾，正是推動天然葬需求的核心動力。天然有機分解（NOR）同時回應了這一缺口的環境與人文兩個層面。

Environmental cost of the status quo現狀的環境代價

The carbon arithmetic does not favour inaction.碳排放的計算，不支持袖手旁觀。

80–230 lbs CO₂ per animal — flame cremation 火化的代價

Cremation requires 760–980°C for several hours using fossil fuels. Most HK pet crematoria operate below the 0.5 t/hr licensing threshold of Cap. 311, meaning zero mandatory emission checks for dioxins, particulates, or nitrogen oxides.

火化需在760至980°C下燃燒數小時。大多數香港寵物火化爐低於第311章的發牌門檻，對二噁英、微粒及氮氧化物排放毫無強制監測。

25× More potent than CO₂ — landfill methane 堆填的甲烷問題

Carcasses sealed in anaerobic plastic bags release methane during decomposition — a gas 25 times more potent than CO₂ over 100 years. This directly contradicts the Waste Blueprint for HK 2035's target to move away from landfill disposal.

遺體在缺氧膠袋中分解，釋放出效力是二氧化碳25倍的甲烷——直接違背《廢物藍圖2035》減少堆填直接棄置的目標。

2,500 kg CO₂e lifetime — large dog 寵物的終身碳足印

A cat generates approximately 310 kg CO₂e per year; an average dog approximately 770 kg. Large dogs can exceed 2,500 kg CO₂e over a lifetime — comparable to two years of a family car's emissions. NOR converts this liability into a verifiable carbon sink.

貓每年產生約310公斤碳當量，狗約770公斤。大型犬終身可超過2,500公斤碳當量。天然有機分解將這份碳債轉化為可驗證的碳匯。

How It Works
運作方式

Natural Organic Reduction — from remains to living soil.

天然有機分解——從遺體到有生命的土壤。

A scientifically validated process already operating in the US, Australia, and Europe
一種已在美國、澳洲及歐洲運作的科學驗證過程

"NOR produces 90% fewer emissions than flame cremation and converts remains into carbon-rich soil — a quantifiable ecological asset."

「天然有機分解較火化少排放90%，並將遺體轉化為富碳土壤——一份可量化的生態資產。若我們開口要求，香港可以做到。」

1
Collection
接送
Home or veterinary clinic · refrigerated transport
由家中或獸醫診所接送 · 冷藏運送

Remains are collected and transported to a licensed NOR facility in the New Territories Control Area — with the care given to a family member.

遺體以冷藏車運往新界管制區的持牌設施，以對待家人的心意妥善照料。

2
Biological transformation
生物轉化
55–75°C · C:N ratio 30:1 · moisture 50% · 4–6 weeks
55–75°C · 碳氮比 30:1 · 濕度 50% · 4至6週

Remains are layered with wood chips and alfalfa straw. Thermophilic microbes raise the internal temperature to 55–75°C for a minimum of 7–10 days, destroying all zoonotic pathogens. A sealed vessel and carbon pad protect groundwater.

嗜熱微生物將溫度提升至55–75°C並維持最少7至10天，消滅所有人畜共患病原體。密封容器及碳墊保護地下水。

3
Soil buffering
土壤緩衝
10:1 compost ratio · pH neutralisation
10:1堆肥比例 · pH中和

Bone minerals are highly alkaline (pH 11.5–12). A mandated 10:1 ratio of active organic compost neutralises toxicity and prevents root shock, producing safe and nutrient-rich soil.

骨礦物質高度鹼性（pH 11.5–12）。以10:1有機堆肥緩衝中和毒性，防止根部受損，產出安全富養分的土壤。

4
A living memorial
生命的紀念
Native species · New Territories forest or home bonsai · GPS-tagged
本地原生物種 · 新界紀念林或家居盆栽 · GPS記錄

The soil nurtures a GPS-tagged native tree in a government-managed memorial forest, or a bonsai for HK's compact homes. Each tree's carbon sequestration is quantified via allometric equations for Climate Action Plan 2050.

土壤孕育帶有GPS記錄的本地原生樹木，種植於政府管理的紀念林或作家居盆栽。每棵樹的固碳量納入《氣候行動藍圖2050》。

Society
社會

Grief is not sentimental. It is biological. It is human. It deserves recognition.

哀傷不是濫情。它是生物本能，是人之常情。它值得被正視。

Clinical psychology, evolutionary biology, and anthropology converge on the same conclusion
臨床心理學、進化生物學與人類學，指向同一結論

Dr Kenneth Doka coined the term disenfranchised grief in 1989 to describe loss that society refuses to validate — loss for which there is no recognised ritual, no bereavement leave, no communal mourning. The death of a pet is the paradigm case. In Hong Kong, research suggests approximately 70% of bereaved owners experience this kind of unacknowledged suffering, further deepened by a cultural taboo around death that makes it hard to speak of at all.

肯尼斯·多卡博士於1989年提出「被剝奪承認的哀傷」——一種社會拒絕認可的喪失感，沒有儀式，沒有喪假，沒有公開哀悼。寵物離世，正是這種哀傷的典型。香港研究顯示，約70%的喪寵飼主承受這種未獲承認的痛苦，加上傳統文化對死亡的禁忌，連開口也難。

What tree burial offers is not merely an ecological service. It offers what psychology calls a rite of passage — a threshold crossed, a grief given form, a loss made into something living. E.O. Wilson's concept of biophilia helps explain the depth of this instinct: we are drawn to life, we need to see life continue. A tree rooted in the soil of a companion is not a metaphor. It is a resolution.

樹葬所提供的，不只是一項生態服務。它提供了心理學所稱的「通過儀禮」——一個被跨越的門檻，一份獲得形式的哀傷，一段失去化為生命延續的旅程。威爾遜的「親生命性」解釋了這種本能的深度：我們渴望生命，我們需要看見生命繼續。一棵紮根於愛寵土壤的樹，不是比喻。它是一個答案。

And this instinct is not unique to us. The biological record shows that mourning — in some form — runs deep across species. Elephants return for years to the bones of their dead. Primate mothers carry deceased infants for weeks. Cetaceans hold their calves at the surface of the sea, refusing to let go. If the capacity for grief is woven into life itself, then providing space to grieve is not indulgence. It is care.

這種本能並非人類獨有。生物學記錄表明，某種形式的哀悼，深植於跨物種的生命之中。大象年復一年回到同伴的骸骨旁。靈長類母親攜帶夭折幼兒長達數週。鯨豚在水面托起死去的幼崽，不肯放手。若哀傷的能力編織於生命之中，那麼為哀傷提供空間，不是縱情，而是關懷。

Policy

No new legislation required. The pathway already exists.

毋需新立法。監管路徑已然存在。

NOR can be accommodated within existing ordinances through strategic reinterpretation
天然有機分解可透過對現行條例的策略性重新詮釋加以規管
Cap. 132 & 354 · Public Health第132及354章 · 公共衛生
The 2025 Amendment as an opportunity
2025年修訂條例的機遇

The 2025 Public Health Amendment raised biosecurity fines to HK$25,000 and tightened enforcement — creating an inhospitable environment for informal burials. NOR's sealed, pest-proof, leak-proof vessels satisfy every biosecurity requirement the Amendment demands, making it the model-compliant technology.

2025年修訂條例收緊生物安全執法，罰款提高至25,000港元。天然有機分解的密封、防蟲、防滲容器恰好完全符合修訂條例的每一項生物安全要求，成為合規的模範技術。

Cap. 139L · AFCD Livestock Framework第139L章 · 漁護署牲畜框架
The livestock licence as a regulatory blueprint
牲畜飼養牌照作為監管藍本

AFCD's Livestock Keeping Licence already divides HK into Prohibition, Control, and Restriction Areas. Control Areas in the New Territories can be designated for NOR facilities, leveraging existing mandates for leak-proof containment and wastewater treatment — without new primary legislation.

漁護署的牲畜飼養牌照已將全港劃分為禁止、管制及限制區域。新界管制區可被指定為天然有機分解設施選址，利用現有的密封容器及廢水處理規定，毋需新的主體法例。

Sustainable Agricultural Development Fund可持續農業發展基金
A funded pilot is already feasible
有資金支持的試點已具可行性

The government's Sustainable Agricultural Development Fund already finances advanced sewage treatment demonstration systems on local farms. This fund could pilot enclosed NOR vessels on existing agricultural land. Financial break-even is estimated at approximately 400 cases per year.

政府的可持續農業發展基金已資助農場的先進污水處理示範系統，理論上可用於試行天然有機分解設備。財務盈虧平衡點估計為每年約400宗。

This website is an extension of a full feasibility study submitted to the Agriculture, Fisheries and Conservation Department (AFCD) as part of an HKU MSc Environmental Management research project.

此網站是香港大學環境管理碩士研究項目的延伸，正式報告已提交予漁農自然護理署，作為一份完整的可行性研究。

The study covers: NOR process validation, emissions modelling (indicative ~409t CO₂e per year under the status quo), legal pathways through Cap. 132, 354, 131 and 139L, indicative economics (break-even approximately 400 cases per year), and pilot recommendations via the Sustainable Agricultural Development Fund.

研究涵蓋：天然有機分解過程驗證、排放模型（現狀估算每年約409噸碳當量）、第132、354、131及139L章的法規路徑、初步經濟分析，以及透過可持續農業發展基金推行試點的建議。

It is a knowledge-translation document, adhering to the government's recognised 1:3:25 model — designed to give policymakers at EPD, FEHD, and AFCD actionable guidance without requiring new primary legislation.

這是一份知識轉化文件，遵循政府認可的1:3:25模型，旨在為各部門提供可行指引，毋需制定新的主體法例。
